- **AC:** 15 (Mage Armor)
- **HP:** 135 (18d8 + 54)    
- **Speed:** 30 ft., Darkvision 120 ft.
- **Saves:** Int +10, Wis +7, Cha +9
- **Resistances:** Cold, Lightning
- **Immunities:** Poison
- **Senses:** Passive Perception 12
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Undercommon, Abyssal
- **Challenge:** 10 (5,900 XP)

**Traits**
- **Spellcasting:** CH 18 (+9 to hit, 9/5/3/3/2 slots). 
  Wichtige Zauber: _[[Dunkelheit]]_, _[[Spinnennetz]]_, _Person festhalten_, _[[Gegenzauber]]_, _[[Person beherrschen]]_, _[[Kältekegel]]_, _[[Telekinese]]_.
- **Innate Spellcasting (DC 16):** Faerie Fire, Darkness 1/day each.
- **Spider Command:** Bonus action: Spawns/quells 1d4 Giant Spiders or Web effects within 60 ft.
- **Leyline Affinity:** Advantage on Arcana checks in forge; +1 spell DC if within 30 ft of Mechanismus.

**Actions**
- **Web Lash (Melee):** +8 vs AC, 3d6+3 poison + restrained (DC 16 STR escape).
- **Arcane Bolt (Ranged):** +10 vs AC, 4d8+5 force (120 ft range, ignores 1/2 cover).

**Legendary Actions (3/round)**
- **Cantrip** (1): Eldritch Blast or similar.
- **Web Trap** (2): 20-ft Web field, DC 16 DEX or restrained.
- **Disengage** (1): Moves 30 ft without OA.

**Taktik während Schmiede-Aktivierung:**
- **Phase 1 (Fern):** Counterspell, Web Lash, Spiders summonen — hält Distanz.
- **Phase 2 (Nahkampf):** Hold Person → Dominate → Telekinesis (Fragment rauben).
- **Flucht:** Dimension Door + Invisibility wenn unter 50 HP.
